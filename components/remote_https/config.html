Tap Scan to look for networks. Your phone may lose the car's hotspot for a few seconds while it scans.

By default the car runs its own hotspot SmartCar (page at https://192.168.4.1/). After you save a network it joins that Wi-Fi instead. If it cannot connect (or after “Forget Wi-Fi”), the SmartCar hotspot comes back about 20 seconds after a restart.

Changing pins restarts the car. Only safe pins for this chip are listed. If a motor turns the wrong way, swap its two pins.

Motor behaviour

Acceleration (0 to full speed) s
Braking (full speed to stop) s
Steering: full power for s
Steering: then hold position at%

Acceleration and braking apply to the forward/back buttons. The steering motor gets full power for a moment to turn the wheels, then drops to the hold power so it does not overheat against the end stop. Phone tilt and the STOP button are not slowed down.
